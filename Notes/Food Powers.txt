X Encounter Power - Type-Based
- Forces a specific type of Pokémon to spawn, if possible. 
- - Level 1: 50% to force spawn. 
- - Level 2: 75% to force spawn. 
- - Level 3: 100% to force spawn.
"All Types" instead boosts the encounter rate by that percentage instead of forcing the encounter to be that type.

X Repel Power - Type-Based 
- Forces a specific type of Pokémon to not spawn, if possible. 
- - Level 1: 50% to force to not spawn. 
- - Level 2: 75% to force to not spawn. 
- - Level 3: 100% to force to not spawn.
"All Types" instead lowers the encounter rate by that percentage instead of forcing the encounter to not be that type.

X Catching Power
- Increases the catch rate in battle. 
- - Level 1: ×1.1 bonus 
- - Level 2: ×1.25 bonus 
- - Level 3: ×2 bonus

X Exp. Point Power - Type-Based
- Increases the Experience received for defeating a specific type of Pokémon.
- - Level 1: ×1.1 bonus 
- - Level 2: ×1.3 bonus 
- - Level 3: ×1.5 bonus

X Base Point Power - Stat-Based
- Increases the EV Points received for defeating Pokémon for specific stats. This stacks with the Power Items, such as 
- - Level 1: +1 bonus
- - Level 2: +2 bonus
- - Level 3: +3 bonus
This has an "All Stats" option.

X Strength Power - Stat-Based
- Increases the IVs of wild Pokémon in a specific stat.
- - Level 1: Scale 15-31 
- - Level 2: Scale 20-31 
- - Level 3: Scale 25-31
This has an "All Stats" option.

X Ability Power
- Increases the chances of wild Pokémon to be found with their Hidden Ability.
- - Level 1: +10% Chance
- - Level 2: +25% Chance
- - Level 3: +50% Chance

X Friendship Power
- Increases the rate at which Pokémon gain happiness and how much friendship wild Pokémon start with. This stacks with the Soothe Bell.
- - Level 1: ×1.1 Wild Starting Happiness, +1 Gain
- - Level 2: ×1.3 Wild Starting Happiness, +2 Gain
- - Level 3: ×1.5 Wild Starting Happiness, +3 Gain

Hatching Power
- Decreases the number of Hatch Cycles required for eggs to hatch.
- - Level 1: -10% Cycles
- - Level 2: -25% Cycles
- - Level 3: -50% Cycles

Daycare Power
- Increases the rate at which Eggs are generated at the Daycare.
- - Level 1: ×5 Egg generation rate
- - Level 2: ×7 Egg generation rate
- - Level 3: ×10 Egg generation rate

Luck Power
- Increases the Rarity Tier for Random Item Loot Tables, stacks with boosting abilities such as Super Luck.
- - Level 1: +1 Tier
- - Level 2: +2 Tiers
- - Level 3: +3 Tiers

Big Haul Power
- Increases the chance for Item Loot Tables to drop 1 additional item.
- - Level 1: 25% increase
- - Level 2: 50% increase
- - Level 3: 100% increase

Item Drop Power - Type-Based
- Increases the chance for a specific type of Pokémon to drop 1 additional item.
- - Level 1: 25% increase
- - Level 2: 50% increase
- - Level 3: 100% increase
This has no effect on specific species and forms.

X Humungo Power - Type-Based
- Increases the chance of large size Pokémon to appear in the wild.
- - Level 1: Scale 128-255
- - Level 2: Scale 160-255
- - Level 3: Scale 192-255

X Teensy Power - Type-Based
- Increases the chance of small size Pokémon to appear in the wild.
- - Level 1: Scale 0-127
- - Level 2: Scale 0-95
- - Level 3: Scale 0-63

Sparkling Power - Type-Based
- Increases the chance for Shiny Pokémon to appear in the wild.
- - Level 1: 1 additional Shiny roll
- - Level 2: 2 additional Shiny rolls
- - Level 3: 3 additional Shiny rolls

Title Power
- Increases the chances of wild Pokémon to generate with a mark.
- - Level 1: 1 additional Mark roll
- - Level 2: 2 additional Mark rolls
- - Level 3: 3 additional Mark rolls

Raid Reward Power
- Increases the item rewards from Raid Battles.
- - Level 1: 1 extra item
- - Level 2: 2 extra items
- - Level 3: 3 extra items

Raid Offense Power
- Boosts the Physical and Special Attack Stats of your Team while in Raid Battles.
- - Level 1: 25% increase
- - Level 2: 50% increase
- - Level 3: 100% increase

Raid Defense Power
- Boosts the Physical and Special Defense Stats of your Team while in Raid Battles.
- - Level 1: 25% increase
- - Level 2: 50% increase
- - Level 3: 100% increase

Raid Speed Power
- Boosts the Speed Stat of your Team while in Raid Battles.
- - Level 1: 25% increase
- - Level 2: 50% increase
- - Level 3: 100% increase

X Fishing Power
- Increases chances of reeling in a Pokémon or Item while fishing.
- - Level 1: ×1.2 Chance
- - Level 2: ×1.5 Chance
- - Level 3: ×2 Chance

X Fishing Item Power
- Increases chances of reeling in an item instead of a Pokémon while fishing.
- - Level 1: ×1.5 Chance
- - Level 2: ×3 Chance
- - Level 3: ×5 Chance
Fishing up an item would use a Loot Table, similar to Rock Smash Rocks or PokéChests.

X Money Power
- Increases the amount of money won at the end of a Trainer Battle. This stacks with moves like Pay Day, Make it Rain, and items like the Amulet Coin.
- - Level 1: 25% Bonus
- - Level 2: 50% Bonus
- - Level 3: 100% Bonus
This does not effect Battle Points won at the end of Battle Facility battles.

Bargaining Power
- Decreases the cost of items at stores.
- - Level 1: 10% Discount
- - Level 2: 25% Discount
- - Level 3: 50% Discount
